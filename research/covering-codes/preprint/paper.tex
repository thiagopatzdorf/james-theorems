\begin{abstract}
We archive eight recovered explicit covering-code constructions and independently
verify their complete ambient spaces by an exact coordinate-wise min-plus transform
and multi-source breadth-first search. The principal construction has 1351 distinct
words over an alphabet of size seven, length nine and covering radius exactly four.
It establishes computational evidence for $K_7(9,4)\le1351$, improving the verified
bound 1475 found in our literature audit by 124 words. Its original-order bytes are
frozen by SHA-256. A packed selector certificate gives an independently checked
radius-four witness for every one of the $7^9=40\,353\,607$ ambient words.
A separately pinned Lean project proves a small explicit construction and the
principal code's cardinality bound, using Florath's existing theory. The principal
coverage certificate has not been replayed in Lean. Validity, formal verification
and global novelty are reported separately; no best-known or world-record claim
is made.
\end{abstract}

\section{Definitions}
Let $A_q=\{0,\ldots,q-1\}$. For $x,c\in A_q^n$ set
$d_H(x,c)=|\{i:x_i\ne c_i\}|$. A finite $C\subseteq A_q^n$ covers at radius
$R$ if $\forall x\,\exists c\in C:d_H(x,c)\le R$.
Write $K_q(n,R)$ for the minimum size of such a code. Its ball volume and sphere
ratio are
\[
 V_q(n,R)=\sum_{i=0}^R\binom ni(q-1)^i,\qquad S=q^n/V_q(n,R),
 \qquad K_q(n,R)\ge\lceil S\rceil.
\]
If $L\le K_q(n,R)\le U$, the normalized quantity
$\alpha=K_q(n,R)/S$ satisfies $L/S\le\alpha\le U/S$; it is not a single
computed value unless $K_q(n,R)$ is known exactly.

\section{Verification protocol}
Verifier A includes a literal exhaustive nearest-codeword oracle for small tests
and a NumPy min-plus transform for large cases. Initialize $D(c)=0$ at codewords
and $D(x)=n+1$ elsewhere. For each coordinate $i$ apply
\[
D_{\mathrm{new}}(x)=\min\{D_{\mathrm{old}}(x),
 1+\min_{a\in A_q}D_{\mathrm{old}}(x_1,\ldots,a,\ldots,x_n)\}.
\]
This is the exact coordinate cost, zero for equality and one for a replacement.
Separability of the sum of coordinate costs proves that after all $n$ transforms,
$D(x)=\min_{c\in C}d_H(x,c)$. The sentinel is above all realizable distances;
intermediate uint8 values are at most $n+2\le34$.

Verifier B, implemented independently in C++, seeds a breadth-first search of
the Hamming graph at all codewords. Each edge changes exactly one coordinate,
so graph distance equals Hamming distance. It visits all $q^n$ packed integer
vertices, independent of the requested radius. It uses checked integer capacity,
signed neighbor arithmetic and a complete-queue invariant. The algorithms share
neither their main traversal nor their hash implementation.

All 255 nonempty binary length-three codes at all four radii are cross-checked
between the literal oracle, transform and BFS; additional seeded tests cover
$q=3,4,5,7,10$. Parser attacks include duplicates, BOM, CRLF, missing newline,
truncated words, invalid digits and control bytes. Reports include raw and unique
cardinality, hash, ambient count, uncovered count and exact covering radius.

The selector certificate stores one uint16 little-endian original-order codeword
index per ambient integer in big-endian base-$q$ order. Its payload is
80\,707\,214 bytes, stored in deterministic gzip. A separate streaming checker
reconstructs every ambient word and checks its selected word's literal Hamming
distance. Truncated, extended, out-of-range and mathematically incorrect witnesses
are rejected even when their hashes are updated.


A compact partition certificate is also generated and checked. Let $L$ be the
343-word linear span described in the archived matrix, and $C_0$ the three-coset
1029-word prefix of the actual file. The checker confirms those complete cosets
and the bijective projection of $L$ to its first three coordinates. Coordinate
translation modulo seven is a Hamming isometry and $C_0+L=C_0$. Each word has
exactly one normalized representative with prefix $000$. A checked witness in
$C_0$ for that representative transports to every word of its orbit. Marked
representatives instead have direct witnesses for every word in the orbit.
The partition therefore exhausts the entire space without a new construction.
There are $7^6=117649$ representatives, with 27 exceptional orbits containing
9261 points. The quotient and exception uint16 payloads total 253820 bytes;
the checker verifies 40\,344\,346 points by symmetry and 9261 explicitly.
This compact route is preferred for future kernel replay; both it and the
original flat full-space certificate are retained. Their external PASS reports
are not substituted for a Lean theorem.

\section{Verified and candidate upper bounds}
\begin{theorem}[Computationally verified construction]
The frozen 1351-word file has 1351 distinct elements of $A_7^9$ and satisfies
$\max_x\min_{c\in C}d_H(x,c)=4$. Consequently $K_7(9,4)\le1351$.
\end{theorem}
\noindent The evidence for this theorem is exhaustive execution and the explicit
object; it is not yet a Lean proof. Both checkers report 40\,353\,607 covered
words and zero uncovered. Every secondary code likewise passes both checkers,
with no duplicates and exact radius equal to its stated $R$.

\section{Lean formal verification}
We pin Florath's library~\cite{florath} at
\texttt{460df105545c2d6b04ba71f29de6b56dbda92825}, using its toolchain
Lean 4.30.0-rc2. \texttt{QaryWord} is \texttt{Fin n}\,$\to$\,\texttt{Fin q};
\texttt{ExplicitQaryUpper} supplies a Finset, cardinality proof and
\texttt{CoversFinset} proof, and \texttt{toUpper} yields \texttt{QaryKUpper}.
No covering-code foundations are reimplemented.

A concurrent research-branch commit also supplies the classical sphere lower bound
$K_7(9,4)\ge221$ via the upstream sphere-covering theorem; this source is
preserved and included in the final build. The actual build proves
$K_2(3,1)\le2$ using $\{000,111\}$ and
\path{covering_decide +kernel}, plus generic selector lemmas and a cardinality
bound for the 1351-word principal Finset loaded in original order. There are zero
\texttt{sorry}, \texttt{admit} or new axiom declarations. Printed dependencies are
only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}.
Native mode is not enabled; upstream native mode overrides the kernel flag if
explicitly enabled. The principal coverage theorem is absent. A generic theorem
requiring an \texttt{ExplicitQaryUpper} hypothesis is not a proof that such an
object exists. The modular-translation isometry, explicit matrix normalization, injectivity, 343-word coset cardinality and coset transport are now proved in Lean. Decoding the actual compact payloads, linking the cosets to the original Finset and reflecting the finite witness checks remain work.

\section{Reproducibility}
Install Python 3.12, NumPy 2.3.5, g++ and OpenSSL headers; install elan for the
pinned nested project, and TeX for the paper. From the repository root:
\begin{lstlisting}[basicstyle=\ttfamily\small]
make covering-fast
make verify-all-computational
make covering-formal
make verify-q7-9-4-1351
make paper
\end{lstlisting}
The principal command checks hashes, both exhaustive verifiers, the packed
certificate and the actual Lean library build. It deliberately returns nonzero
until an unconditional principal theorem is replayed; successful computational
reproduction is available separately via \path{make verify-computational}.
PR CI uses fast checks and the small/formal library; exhaustive checking is manual,
not run on every push. CI results are not claimed until the workflow executes.
The original cache download failed HTTP 403/429; the actual Lean dependencies
were built from source. \texttt{lake update} succeeds with the upstream-supported
\path{MATHLIB_NO_CACHE_ON_UPDATE=1} setting.

\section{Limitations}
The computational trusted base includes Python/NumPy, C++/OpenSSL, their compilers
and the operating system; testing is not a proof of all executable semantics.
The principal selector has external validation but no kernel replay. Generator
revision/seed is incompletely recorded. Novelty review covered primary arXiv texts,
ancillaries, historical tables, citation paths, Exa and Crossref; Google Scholar
returned HTTP 429, zbMATH and MathSciNet HTTP 403. A complete literature or
forward-citation search is not claimed. Secondary novelty remains unresolved,
including potential older linear covering constructions. No assertion is made
that 1351 is optimal or that a 1350-word search has been exhausted.

\section{Conclusion}
The explicit object and two independent exhaustive algorithms support the eight
stated upper bounds. The principal construction improves the verified 1475 bound
found in this audit. The formal gap is precisely coverage replay for the frozen
principal object, rather than construction recovery or executable coverage.
